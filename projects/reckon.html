Reckon

  • Brand Identity
  • Product Design

Reckon is a money app built for my own household. It never connects to a bank; it only knows what you choose to tell it. The identity centres on an impossible balance: a mark that wobbles, falls and restacks itself every time the app opens.

One colour, flat. Faces turned from the light are solid.
Mark

An impossible balance

Most of us were never taught how money works, and quietly assume everyone else was. So the bills, the balances and the what-ifs build into a low, constant hum of stress, and asking for help feels like admitting you’re behind. Reckon starts somewhere kinder: you don’t have to be good at money to keep it in balance.

Sphere

What you’re protecting

The nest egg: card paydown, savings, retirement. The largest form, carried on top.

Cube

Your unit

The fixed monthly needs, balanced on a single corner. The more cubic, the more stable.

Cone

Automate the base

Money flowing in and being directed: transfers and autopays on autopilot. A broad base whose tip carries everything.
